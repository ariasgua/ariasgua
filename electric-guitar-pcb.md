# 🎸 Custom Electric Guitar PCB

**Technologies:** LM324, analog circuits, PCB design, through-hole and SMD assembly

## Overview

A guitar-shaped analog effects system built around LM324 amplification, filtering, signal conditioning, and interactive LEDs.

## Engineering work

- Translated schematics into a physical PCB and assembled the hardware.
- Used through-hole and SMD soldering to populate the board.
- Measured signals, isolated faults, and iterated on the design during bench debugging.

Public schematic and layout files have not been linked here yet.
